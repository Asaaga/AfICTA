Who We Are

Uniting Africa's ICT Ecosystem

The Africa Information & Communication Technologies Alliance (AfICTA) is a continental body representing ICT associations, companies, and institutions from across all 54 African nations.

We advocate for policies that advance Africa's digital economy, connect stakeholders across borders, and build the capacity needed to ensure Africa fully participates in the global information society.
