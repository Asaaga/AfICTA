13th AfICTA Summit — 2025

The 13th AfICTA Annual Summit brought together Africa's most influential ICT leaders, policymakers, and innovators to address the continent's most pressing digital challenges and opportunities.

Themed around Africa's accelerating digital transformation, the summit produced actionable communiqués and strategic commitments that are guiding AfICTA's work through 2026 and beyond.

Opportunity

Host an AfICTA Summit

Hosting an AfICTA Annual Summit positions your country or organisation at the forefront of Africa's digital transformation conversation. Past host cities include Abuja, Cairo, Johannesburg, Windhoek, and Nairobi.

Summit hosts benefit from extensive continental and international media coverage, networking with heads of state, ministers, and over 500 ICT leaders, as well as the opportunity to shape the pan-African digital agenda.

Express Your Interest
What Hosting Includes
  • 500+ international delegates
  • Pan-African media coverage
  • Policy declaration in your city's name
  • Alignment with government digital agenda
  • Full AfICTA organisational support
