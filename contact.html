Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

You can join AfICTA by completing the membership application form on our Membership page. Select the appropriate membership category (National Association, Corporate, or Individual), fill out the details, and our team will be in touch within 3 business days.

Registration details for upcoming summits and webinars are published on the Events page and the Webinars page. You can also contact us directly at info@aficta.org to express interest in attending or presenting.

Expressions of interest for hosting an AfICTA Annual Summit are welcome from member nations and organisations. Visit the Host a Summit section or contact us directly to discuss your proposal, timelines, and the support AfICTA provides to host cities.

AfICTA accepts payment online via our secure Pay Online portal, or by bank transfer. Contact us at info@aficta.org for bank transfer details or alternative payment arrangements suitable to your country or organisation.

Media enquiries should be directed to info@aficta.org with the subject line "MEDIA REQUEST". Please include your name, media organisation, and the nature of your request. Our communications team aims to respond to media enquiries within one business day.
