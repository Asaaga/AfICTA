AfICTA Elects New Regional Vice-Chairs to Strengthen Continental Representation

The Africa ICT Alliance (AfICTA) has elected Laïcana Coulibaly as Vice-Chair for West Africa and Olawale Osborne Oderinde as Vice-Chair for Southern Africa, following a rigorous selection process. This leadership expansion reinforces AfICTA's commitment to equitable regional representation and stronger advocacy across the continent.

Both newly elected Vice-Chairs bring deep expertise in digital policy, regional ICT governance, and multi-stakeholder engagement — essential capabilities as AfICTA navigates an increasingly complex global digital landscape.
